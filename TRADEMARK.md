The name **Ellenial™**, the Ellenial flower logo and app names that include Ellenial are not covered by that licence. They belong to me, Ellen Nilsson, and show that something is made by me and follows the Ellenial promise: free, private, no ads, no trackers.

**You're welcome to**
- fork this code and publish your own version under your own name and logo
- say your project is "based on Ellenial" or "a fork of [app name]"
- write about the apps, link to them and use the name to refer to them

**Please don't**
- publish a copy or a fork under the Ellenial name or with the flower logo
- use the name or logo in a way that suggests I made, run or approve your version
- use the name in app store listings, domains or social accounts for your own project
